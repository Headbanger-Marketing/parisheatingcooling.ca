About Us

HVAC Service Rooted in Paris & the Grand River Valley

From cobblestone heritage homes along the Grand to newer builds up the hill, we keep Paris and the surrounding Brant/Haldimand-Norfolk region comfortable when the Lake Erie wind bites in January and the humidity settles in for July.

Our Story

Our Story on the Grand River

Paris sits in a bend of the Grand River, and that geography shapes how homes here actually heat and cool. We built this company around the real housing stock of our town, not a generic Ontario template.

Our shop on Grand River Street North is a short walk from the downtown limestone buildings that earned Paris its reputation as one of Canada's prettiest little towns. Those thick stone walls hold heat differently than the vinyl-sided subdivisions creeping out past the bypass, and neither one behaves like the century farmhouses scattered through Brant and Haldimand-Norfolk. Sizing a furnace, a heat pump, or a ductless mini-split for each of them is a different conversation, and we have it every single day.

Winters here arrive on the wind off Lake Erie and settle in for months. Summers bring the opposite problem, the kind of lake-driven humidity that makes 26 degrees feel like 32 and pushes an undersized air conditioner into non-stop cycling. Whether you live in a drafty riverside cottage in Paris, a post-war bungalow in Brantford, or a newer build near Caledonia or Simcoe, the equipment has to match the home and the season, not just get swapped in.

We are a family-run shop, and we answer the phone ourselves. No call centre, no subcontractor chain, no voice menu, just the same team from the first quote through the final walkthrough.

Built for the Grand River Valley

Heritage stone, century wood-frame, and brand-new construction all heat and cool differently. We size every system to the actual building sitting in front of us, so your furnace holds through a cold snap and your AC keeps up when the Lake Erie humidity rolls inland.

Our Commitment

How We Approach Every Job

We service what we install, and we only install what your home genuinely needs. That covers high-efficiency furnace repair and installation, central and ductless air conditioning, heat pumps, smart and programmable thermostats, fireplace installation, and duct cleaning, the full scope of residential HVAC work for the mixed housing stock across Paris, Brantford, Caledonia, Dunnville, Simcoe, Port Dover, Nanticoke, Ayr, and Aylmer.

Every visit starts with a real assessment of the building, its age, insulation, ductwork, and how your family actually uses the space, before any equipment is recommended. You get a written quote, a plain-language explanation, and the same technicians from start to finish. We pull permits where required, follow TSSA and ESA requirements, and stand behind the work long after the truck leaves your driveway.
